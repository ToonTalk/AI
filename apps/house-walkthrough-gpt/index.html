ABOUT THE WALKTHROUGH

From drawings to spaces.

This model interprets the proposed house in your 24 September 2026 building drawings: ground and first floor plans (101 / 102, revision B), roof plan (103, revision B), elevations (200 / 201), sections (300 / 301), and the window and door schedules.

The two front bays, rear kitchen / dining extension, bedrooms, studies, utility and bathrooms follow those drawings. Key dimensions include the 8.01 m main frontage, 7.073 m rear extension width, and 2.485 m dining sliding-door opening.

Finishes and furniture are illustrative. Several colours, floor finishes, window frames and the kitchen design are marked to be confirmed in the documents. Room geometry, existing dimensions, roof intersections and the garden are simplified for this visual model. It is an approximate preview, not a measured construction model.
